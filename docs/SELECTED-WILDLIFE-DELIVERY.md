## Fidelity and limits

Eight derived near/far meshes preserve rig/clip/image bytes and material bindings. Skin weights are interpolated after reduction, tangents may be regenerated, and shark detached parts receive a documented coordinate repair. Not pixel-identical author files; originals remain untouched.

Turtle cornea preserves its original factors and genuine optical contribution using an existing opaque-colour/depth render, avoiding a second whole-island render. Tiny silhouette/intersection filtering differences remain; see OWNER-LENS-RENDER.md. No scenery or eye parts were removed to meet budgets.

Native land still has measured turn/transition sliding and small carrier support motion. Tiger's slow bounded patrol is qualified, not arbitrary detours, sprint/gallop, weather shelter or broad roaming. Marine surface approach does not certify anatomical blowhole contact/spouts. Whale/school/turtle lack <5k far variants. No native FPS, mobile smoothness or full-guide acceptance is claimed.
