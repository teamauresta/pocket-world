About

Pocket World

The terrain, glass-sided sea and plants are built in code with Three.js and WebGL. The selected supplied wildlife uses native model shapes, rigs, materials and motion where available. Tiger walking, added whale propulsion, static fish/turtle motion and eagle wing articulation are explicitly generated adaptations, not newly discovered source clips. Stag/deer remain removed. The world retains existing birds, tuna and manta alongside the new land and marine species.

Drag to turn the diorama, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag or two-finger drag to pan. Tap an animal to learn about it and follow it around.

Weather changes on its own; tap the cloud button to choose it.


Changing detail reloads the island. All animal types are retained.

Supplied-model licences include non-commercial restrictions. This is a private preview, not clearance for a public or commercial release.

Manta: Model 84B — DigitalLife3D, CC-BY-NC 4.0. Unmodified supplied model; runtime scale, animation and underwater shading adapted. Private non-commercial preview only.
